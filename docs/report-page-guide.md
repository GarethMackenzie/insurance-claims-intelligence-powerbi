# Report page guide

These captions explain the management purpose of each page. The repository currently provides labelled design mockups; genuine Power BI Desktop screenshots should replace them as primary evidence only after runtime verification.

## 1. Executive Claims Overview

**Caption:** Portfolio-level view of claim volume, incurred exposure, paid amounts, reserve, severity, SLA performance and emerging management exceptions.

**Decision supported:** Where is portfolio pressure building, and which financial or service indicator requires attention first?

## 2. Claims Operations

**Caption:** Operational view of lifecycle delay, backlog ageing, documentation and supplier queues, settlement performance and SLA movement.

**Decision supported:** Which open-claim stage or dependency should receive capacity and barrier-removal attention?

## 3. Risk & Review Intelligence

**Caption:** Capacity-constrained review prioritization showing how limited investigation resources can focus on higher-risk synthetic claims while retaining human judgement.

**Decision supported:** What queue size is operationally feasible, and how does that choice change target coverage, precision and non-target review volume?

## 4. Financial Performance

**Caption:** Financial view of incurred cost, paid amounts, outstanding reserve, severity mix, large-loss exposure and product or regional concentration.

**Decision supported:** Which portfolio segment contributes most to current financial exposure and warrants deeper review?

## 5. Regional Intelligence

**Caption:** South African province-level comparison of volume, incurred exposure, severity, settlement time, SLA and high-risk referrals.

**Decision supported:** Where do cost, service or risk indicators materially differ after considering portfolio mix?

## 6. Handler Performance

**Caption:** Balanced workload-management view combining volume, open inventory, service, quality and case-complexity context.

**Decision supported:** Where should work be rebalanced or operational support added without treating the view as an automated employee judgement?

## 7. Root Cause Analysis

**Caption:** Guided decomposition of a selected metric across product, claim type, region, severity band, channel and time.

**Decision supported:** Which segment contributes most to a change, and what should be investigated before drawing a causal conclusion?

## 8. Data Quality

**Caption:** Control view of seeded issue categories, severity, ingestion batches, affected stages, remediation outcomes and data-quality score.

**Decision supported:** Which source defect creates the greatest analytical risk, and has the governed correction pipeline detected it completely?

## Presentation order

For recruiters and hiring managers, lead with Executive Claims Overview, then Architecture, Claims Operations, Risk & Review Intelligence, and finish with the QA evidence. Use the remaining pages to answer role-specific questions.
